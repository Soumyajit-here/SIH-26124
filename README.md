# BharatPotHole — AI-Powered Fleet-Scale Road Hazard Intelligence

**Team VEGA | Smart India Hackathon 2026 | SIH26124**

BharatPotHole is an AI-powered road-condition monitoring platform that transforms public-transport buses into **mobile urban sensing units**. A front-facing camera and GPS-equipped edge device continuously analyze road scenes, generate geotagged observations, and transmit lightweight detection data to a centralized platform.

The platform combines observations from multiple buses into persistent road-hazard events and visualizes them through a live GIS dashboard.

---

## 🚧 Problem

Municipal road inspections often rely on:

- Manual surveys
- Fixed CCTV cameras
- Citizen complaints
- Periodic inspections

These approaches provide incomplete and delayed information about changing road conditions.

Public buses, however, already travel across large portions of a city every day. BharatPotHole uses this existing fleet as a **distributed road-sensing network**.
